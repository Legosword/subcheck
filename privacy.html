Privacy

SubCheck stores the subscription entries you add locally in your browser using local storage. Your subscription names, prices, and usage information are not sent to SubCheck servers.

SubCheck uses Google Analytics to understand general site usage, such as page visits and feature interactions. We do not intentionally send your subscription names or exact prices to Google Analytics.

You can remove your saved subscription data at any time by using the Clear All Data button on the calculator.
